For My Love

When most I wink, then do mine eyes best see,
For all the day they view things unrespected;
But when I sleep, in dreams they look on thee,
And, darkly bright, are bright in dark directed.
If distance bars my steps from where thou art,
Then let the wind be messenger of me;
For every beat that stirs within my heart
Cries out to cross the space 'tween thee and me.

No star so far, no ocean wide nor deep,
Could chain my will, nor bind my longing soul;
For thoughts of thee invade both wake and sleep,
And in thy absence, I am but not whole.

O grant me but a moment in thy sight,
To stand where once thy gentle presence lay;
For one brief glance would turn my dark to light,
And steal my restless yearning all away.

Nagpapasalamat talaga ako na saken ka nagagalit, naiinis, nagtatampo, at umiiyak, pero kahit na ganon nalalampasan parin nating dalawa ang problema. Kahit na ganun, nalalampasan pa rin natin ang mga problema nang magkasama. Sobrang mahalaga sa akin na kahit may tampuhan, hindi tayo nawawala sa isa't isa. Iba talaga yung connection natin kasi kahit may away, andyan pa rin yung pagmamahal at respeto na hindi madaling matanggal.

Alam ko na minsan mahirap, pero ang ganda ng samahan natin dahil kahit may mga pagsubok, hindi tayo sumusuko. Sa bawat tampo at iyak mo, natututo akong maging mas maunawain at mas matatag na partner. Hindi perfect ang relationship natin, pero sa bawat pagsubok, mas lumalalim ang pagmamahal at pagtitiwala natin sa isa't isa.
